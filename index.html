中文版

概述

「快死了」（Dying Soon）尊重并保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

数据收集

我们的应用仅在您的设备本地存储以下信息：

  • 您输入的出生日期
  • 您设置的预期寿命
  • 语言偏好设置
  • 推送通知设置

数据存储

出生日期、预期寿命、所选屏幕使用时间应用及专注行动记录保存在您的设备上。我们不会将这些内容发送给分析服务。购买相关数据的处理方式见下文。

推送通知

如果您启用推送通知，通知内容完全在本地生成，不涉及任何网络传输。

第三方服务

我们使用 Apple StoreKit 和 RevenueCat 验证购买、恢复 Pro 权限并统计购买转化。RevenueCat 会处理随机生成的应用用户标识、商品和交易信息，以及购买页展示事件（含展示时间和购买页版本），用于应用功能和分析。我们不接收银行卡信息，不出售这些数据，也不用于跨应用广告跟踪。详情见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。如需查询或删除相关记录，请通过下方邮箱联系我们；我们可能需要您的 RevenueCat 应用用户标识以定位记录。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

隐私政策变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在此页面发布，并更新「最后更新」日期。

联系我们

如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系：lain0911@126.com

English Version

Overview

"Dying Soon" respects and protects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Data Collection

Our app only stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Your birth date
  • Your life expectancy setting
  • Language preferences
  • Push notification settings

Data Storage

Your birth date, life expectancy, selected Screen Time apps, and focus action history stay on your device. We do not send this content to analytics services. Purchase-related processing is described below.

Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, all notification content is generated locally without any network transmission.

Third-Party Services

We use Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore Pro access, and measure purchase conversion. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier, product and transaction information, and paywall impression events (including their time and paywall version) for app functionality and analytics. We do not receive payment card details, sell this data, or use it for cross-app advertising tracking. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. To request access to or deletion of these records, contact us below; we may need your RevenueCat app user identifier to locate them.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us
